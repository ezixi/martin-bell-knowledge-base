# References and Recommendations

These recommendations provide third-party perspectives from managers, direct
reports, peers and senior stakeholders I have worked with.

They are included as independent supporting evidence for the experience and
projects described elsewhere in this repository.

## Newport — Joe Macari

**Relationship:** Direct manager  
**Role:** Vice President Data Analytics  
**Recommendation:** June 2022

> I hired Martin into our team as a Lead Data Engineer. As soon as Martin came
> in he completely transformed how we managed our data as well as how we
> processed the data we collected. This led to us being a much more efficient
> and well documented team which comes along with its obvious benefits.
>
> Away from the engineering side, Martin also has a great knowledge of technical
> solutions which got us out of a couple of sticky situations. He is a tireless
> worker when needed which at one point included rebuilding a server bit by bit.
>
> Martin is fantastic to work with and can translate complex technical
> requirements to non-technically minded people (or not so technically minded
> people) which was very valuable when dealing with junior members of the team
> as well as our stakeholders.
>
> I learnt a lot from Martin in our time working together, as well as our coffee
> shop get togethers during the less strict parts of lockdown, and I am very
> grateful for the knowledge he imparted. Any business that Martin works for in
> the future will be very fortunate to have him, and a data warehouse/pipelines
> organised to the highest standard, of that I have no doubt!

- Transformed an existing data capability.
- Improved engineering efficiency and documentation.
- Strong hands-on technical problem solving.
- Able to communicate complex technology to non-technical stakeholders.
- Developed and coached less experienced colleagues.
- Established high standards for warehouses and pipelines.

## Cross-reference themes

### Leadership

Recommendations from both managers and direct reports describe my ability to
define direction, build teams, maintain high expectations and care about the
people doing the work.

See: Zachary Campau, Jon Simpson.

### Hands-on technical leadership

Multiple recommendations independently describe me as a leader who remains
technically involved rather than managing purely through delegation.

See: Zachary Campau, Zackary Walker, Jon Simpson, Joe Macari.

### Developing people

Recommendations describe hands-on coaching, sharing technical knowledge,
encouraging people to learn, running open office hours and helping both
technical and non-technical colleagues become more capable.

See: Joe Macari, Zackary Walker, Jon Simpson, Peter Collingridge.

### Communication

Several people independently highlight my ability to translate technical or
analytical concepts for people without the same technical background.

See: Joe Macari, Zackary Walker, Peter Collingridge.

### Organisational transformation

Recommendations from different companies describe transformation rather than
simply delivery within an existing environment.

At Stonecrop, this meant turning a back-office data service into a strategic
data science and engineering organisation.

At Safari, it meant helping the company become more data-driven and
data-literate.

At Newport, it meant transforming how the organisation managed and processed
its data.

See: Zachary Campau, Peter Collingridge, Joe Macari.

### Constructive challenge

The Safari Product recommendation provides explicit evidence that I am willing
to challenge senior stakeholders when I believe they are interpreting the data
incorrectly, while continuing to work with them to answer the underlying
business question.

See: Peter Collingridge.
