# How to Choose the Best App Analytics Tools

An article I wrote for ArcTouch as part of the content programme developed around *The Smart App*, a comprehensive guide to mobile analytics.

The article examines how organisations should approach the selection of mobile analytics tools. Rather than treating tooling as the starting point, the broader approach was to first understand the business objectives, KPIs and measurement requirements of the product and then select technology capable of supporting them.

The article formed part of a wider content strategy in which material from the ebook was published as standalone, searchable articles while the complete guide acted as a downloadable lead-generation asset.

## Why it is relevant

The article demonstrates an approach that has remained consistent throughout my later data work: technology selection should follow requirements rather than drive them.

It also provides evidence of my experience translating analytics concepts into practical guidance for product and development audiences.
