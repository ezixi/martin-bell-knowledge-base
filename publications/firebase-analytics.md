# Firebase Analytics Heats Up Google's App Toolset

An article I wrote for ArcTouch examining Firebase Analytics and Google's developing mobile application analytics ecosystem.

The article formed part of the wider content programme surrounding *The Smart App* and provided a more technology-specific companion to the guide's broader discussion of analytics strategy and tool selection.

## Why it is relevant

The article provides evidence of my hands-on background in digital and product analytics and my ability to evaluate emerging analytics technologies in the context of wider measurement requirements.

It also sits alongside my earlier work implementing Google Analytics and developing measurement strategies for digital products at Safari/O'Reilly.
