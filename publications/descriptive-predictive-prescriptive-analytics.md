# Predictive, Prescriptive and Descriptive Analytics for Apps

An article I wrote for ArcTouch explaining the relationship between descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics in the context of mobile products.

The article was adapted from *The Smart App* as part of ArcTouch's wider content programme.

The framework moves analytics beyond simply reporting what has already happened:

- **Descriptive analytics** helps establish what happened.
- **Predictive analytics** uses existing evidence to understand what may happen.
- **Prescriptive analytics** helps determine what action should be taken.

The material also connects analysis with hypothesis-driven experimentation, including A/B and multivariate testing, so that proposed actions can be tested and their results incorporated into subsequent decisions.

## Why it is relevant

The article demonstrates my view of analytics as a decision-making discipline rather than a reporting function.

Analysis creates the most value when it results in a hypothesis, decision or intervention whose outcome can subsequently be measured.
