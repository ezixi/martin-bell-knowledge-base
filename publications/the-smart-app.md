# The Smart App

A comprehensive guide to using analytics throughout the lifecycle of a mobile
application, commissioned and published by ArcTouch in 2016.

I wrote the guide as part of a content marketing and lead-generation project
developed with ArcTouch's marketing team.

The central argument is that analytics should be designed around business
objectives rather than added to a product after launch. The guide works from
business objectives and KPIs through measurement planning, segmentation,
experimentation and optimisation.

Topics include:

- defining business objectives and KPIs;
- measurement planning;
- the One Metric That Matters;
- segmentation and cohort analysis;
- descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics;
- A/B and multivariate experimentation;
- building an analytics culture;
- shared metric definitions and data dictionaries;
- analytics tooling;
- customer lifetime value and acquisition cost;
- attribution;
- mobile app ROI.

Material from the ebook was also published by ArcTouch as a series of standalone
articles for search, social distribution and lead generation.

## Why it is relevant

The publication captures many principles that have remained consistent throughout
my later work in analytics and data leadership: begin with business objectives,
define shared measures of success, make data understandable across the
organisation, test hypotheses, and use measurement to drive decisions rather
than simply report what happened.
