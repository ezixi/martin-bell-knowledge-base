# What's My Mobile App ROI?

An article I wrote for ArcTouch examining how organisations can use analytics to understand the commercial return generated by mobile applications.

The article was adapted from *The Smart App* as part of a wider content marketing and lead-generation programme.

It considers mobile performance in commercial rather than purely behavioural terms, including the relationship between customer acquisition cost, customer lifetime value, segmentation, attribution and return on investment.

## Why it is relevant

The article demonstrates that my analytics work has consistently connected customer behaviour with commercial outcomes.

Rather than treating measures such as downloads, sessions or engagement as ends in themselves, the objective is to understand how customer behaviour ultimately contributes to business value.

That same principle has subsequently informed my work across product analytics, marketing effectiveness, LTV and data strategy.
