# How to Use Mobile Analytics to Drive Your App Strategy

An article I wrote for ArcTouch about using analytics as an input into mobile product strategy.

It was developed from *The Smart App* and published as part of the accompanying content marketing programme.

The central idea is that analytics should begin before instrumentation or tool selection. Organisations should establish what the product is intended to achieve, identify measurable objectives and KPIs, and then design measurement around those questions.

Analytics then becomes part of a feedback loop: measure behaviour, evaluate results against objectives, develop hypotheses and use what is learned to influence subsequent product decisions.

## Why it is relevant

This article captures one of the principles that has remained central to my work:

> Start with the decision or business problem, not the data or technology.

It provides an early example of the approach I later applied more broadly to analytics strategy, data strategy, KPI design and semantic modelling.
