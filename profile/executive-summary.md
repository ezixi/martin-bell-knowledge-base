# Executive Summary

## Who I Am

I am an analytics and data leader with experience building teams, designing modern data platforms, and helping organisations use data to make better decisions.
My background spans analytics engineering, data engineering, business intelligence, product analytics, data strategy, and organisational leadership.

Throughout my career I have worked across digital media, e-commerce, logistics, sports technology and gaming, delivering both technical platforms and
organisational transformation. My focus has consistently been on creating trusted data, developing high-performing teams, and ensuring analytics delivers
measurable business value.

## What I Specialise In

My areas of expertise include:

- Building and leading Analytics Engineering and Data teams.
- Designing modern cloud-based analytics platforms.
- Developing data strategies aligned with business objectives.
- Establishing governance, engineering standards and delivery processes.
- Creating trusted business metrics and reporting.
- Partnering with executive stakeholders to translate business problems into data solutions.
- Preparing organisations for AI-driven analytics through strong data foundations.

## Leadership Philosophy

I believe successful data organisations are built by combining strong engineering practices with deep business understanding.

Technology alone rarely solves organisational data problems. Lasting improvements come from clear ownership, well-defined processes, high-quality
data, and teams that understand both the technical and commercial context of their work.

My leadership style focuses on building collaborative, learning-oriented teams that are empowered to solve business problems rather than simply deliver
technical outputs.

## Career Highlights

Highlights from my career include:

- Built and led Analytics Engineering, Insights and Data Science functions.
- Designed and modernised enterprise data platforms using technologies including Snowflake, BigQuery and Azure.
- Led large-scale platform migrations and reporting modernisation programmes.
- Reduced operational data quality issues through improved governance and engineering practices.
- Delivered analytics products supporting executive decision making across product, marketing, finance and operations.
- Developed data strategies that transformed reporting from manual processes into scalable organisational capabilities.
- Introduced experimentation, product analytics and customer acquisition optimisation programmes that improved commercial performance.
- Advised organisations on cloud modernisation, analytics architecture and AI readiness.

## Core Principles

Several themes are consistent throughout my career:

### Build trusted data

Reliable analytics begins with high-quality engineering, governance and clearly defined business concepts.

### Solve business problems

Technology is a means to an end. Every platform, process and dashboard should exist because it enables better business decisions.

### Develop people

Strong teams outperform individual experts. Investing in mentoring, coaching and knowledge sharing creates sustainable organisations.

### Think strategically, deliver pragmatically

I prefer iterative delivery with a clear long-term vision rather than pursuing large-scale transformation without measurable business value.

### Enable the future

Modern data platforms should not only support reporting today, but also provide the foundations for advanced analytics, automation and AI.
