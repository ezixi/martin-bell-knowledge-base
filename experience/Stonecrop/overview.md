# Stonecrop Technologies

## Summary

As Senior Manager, Data, I was responsible for transforming data from a collection of disconnected reporting processes into a strategic organisational capability. I established the vision, operating model and technical direction for the company's data function, building scalable analytics platforms that supported large-scale logistics operations and enabled faster, more informed business decisions.

The role combined strategic leadership with hands-on technical architecture, requiring close collaboration with development teams, operations and executive stakeholders.

## Objectives

The primary objectives of the role were to:

- Establish a scalable organisational data strategy.
- Replace fragmented manual reporting processes.
- Build analytics platforms capable of supporting operational decision making.
- Improve data quality and governance.
- Enable near real-time reporting for business-critical operations.

## Responsibilities

My responsibilities included:

- Defining and executing the organisation's data strategy.
- Leading the design and development of analytics platforms.
- Establishing governance processes and delivery standards.
- Introducing Agile practices into data delivery.
- Working with business stakeholders to prioritise analytics initiatives.
- Delivering data products supporting operational and strategic planning.

## Major achievements

Key achievements included:

- Developed and implemented a company-wide data strategy that transformed manual reporting into a scalable analytics function.
- Introduced Agile delivery practices that improved delivery predictability and collaboration.
- Established governance processes that improved data quality and operational consistency.
- Delivered analytics products supporting billion-dollar network deployment decisions.
- Reduced manufacturing backlog processing time from weeks to minutes through workflow automation and operational analytics.

## Leadership

This role represented my first opportunity to build and lead a strategic data capability. Success depended on defining a clear vision, aligning stakeholders around a common roadmap, and creating delivery processes that balanced technical quality with business value. It required influencing across engineering, operations and executive leadership while developing a culture of continuous improvement.
