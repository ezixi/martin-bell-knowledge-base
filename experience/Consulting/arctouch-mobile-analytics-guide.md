# The Smart App: Mobile Analytics Content Strategy

During my independent consulting period, I was commissioned by ArcTouch to develop a substantial piece of thought-leadership content around mobile analytics.

 Across a series of meetings with the client we developed the idea of creating a comprehensive guide to mobile analytics that could serve several purposes at once:

- provide a downloadable ebook for lead generation;
- create a series of individual articles for organic search;
- provide useful material for social distribution;
- demonstrate ArcTouch's expertise in mobile strategy and analytics.

The result was *The Smart App: A Comprehensive Guide for Using Mobile Analytics to Make Your App a Success — Right from the Start*.

## What I did

I developed and wrote the analytics content, translating my experience in digital, product and customer analytics into material aimed at product teams and organisations developing mobile applications.

Rather than concentrating narrowly on analytics tooling, I structured the material around the idea that measurement should begin with business and customer objectives.

The guide covered:

- defining objectives, KPIs and success measures;
- creating measurement plans;
- identifying the "One Metric That Matters";
- segmentation and cohort analysis;
- descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics;
- hypothesis-driven experimentation;
- creating an analytics culture;
- establishing common metric definitions and a data dictionary;
- selecting analytics and optimisation tooling;
- customer lifetime value and acquisition cost;
- attribution and segmentation;
- measuring and improving ROI.

A central argument was that analytics should be designed into a product from the beginning rather than added after launch. Product features should connect to measurable objectives, and the resulting data should create a feedback loop through which teams learn and improve the product.

## Content marketing strategy

The ebook was deliberately designed as more than a standalone publication.

The material could be decomposed into articles addressing specific search and customer questions. Those articles created indexable SEO content and material that could be distributed through social channels, while the complete ebook provided a lead-generation asset.

That gave ArcTouch a simple content funnel:
**Search / social → useful specialist content → ebook → lead generation**

The strategy also meant that one substantial piece of research and writing could generate multiple reusable marketing assets rather than disappearing after a single campaign.

ArcTouch published the ebook in 2016 and subsequently published material from it as individual articles. Much of that content remains publicly available years later.

## Why this project matters

This project sits at an interesting point in my career because it brought together areas that can otherwise look separate on a CV: analytics, product strategy, commercial thinking and communication.

I wasn't simply explaining how to use analytics software. I was articulating a broader approach:
**start with the business problem, define what success means, design measurement around it, create shared definitions, test hypotheses and use the results to decide what to do next.**

That same approach subsequently became central to how I approached analytics strategy, metric governance, semantic layers and data platforms.
