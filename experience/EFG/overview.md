# ESL FACEIT Group

## Summary

As Manager of Analytics Engineering & Insights, I led multidisciplinary data teams spanning Analytics Engineering, Insights and Data Science. My role combined technical leadership, organisational design, stakeholder management and the delivery of data products that enabled better business decision-making across a global organisation.

## Responsibilities

My responsibilities included:

- Building and leading analytics engineering capabilities.
- Designing a comprehensive modelling and semantic layer to power reporting and AI conversational analytics.
- Partnering with product, marketing, finance and business stakeholders to define trusted KPIs.
- Driving improvements to data quality and governance.
- Modernising the analytics platform.
- Developing analysts and analytics engineers across multiple seniority levels.
- Establishing processes that improved reliability, documentation and delivery.

## Major achievements

Key achievements during my time included:

- Creating a dedicated Analytics Engineering function.
- Reducing costs by leading the migration from BigQuery to Snowflake.
- Modernising business reporting through Tableau.
- Reducing data quality issues from multiple per day to zero.
- Reducing time-to-insight from weeks to hours.
- Introducing a data helpdesk process that reduced average ticket age from around 70 days to under one week.
- Delivering commercial, marketing and product insights that improved customer lifetime value, marketing efficiency and advertising performance.

## Leadership

I managed people across Analytics Engineering, Data Analysis and Data Science, hiring, mentoring and developing engineers and analysts from junior through to principal level while promoting a collaborative, learning-focused culture.
