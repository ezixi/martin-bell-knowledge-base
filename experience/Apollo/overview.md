# Apollo MIS

## Summary

As a Data Architecture & Analytics Consultant, I was engaged to assess the existing analytics platform of a rapidly growing sports technology company and define a strategy for its next stage of growth. Rather than delivering an isolated technical solution, the engagement focused on understanding the organisation's current architecture, identifying operational and technical risks, and producing a pragmatic roadmap for a modern analytics platform.

The outcome was a strategic architecture that improved scalability, strengthened governance, reduced infrastructure costs, and established a foundation for future analytics engineering and AI initiatives.

## Objectives

The engagement focused on:

- Reviewing the existing Azure-based data platform and reporting architecture.
- Identifying opportunities to improve performance, reliability and maintainability.
- Reducing infrastructure and operational costs.
- Establishing analytics engineering best practices.
- Defining a target architecture that could scale with the business.
- Assessing the organisation's readiness for AI and LLM-based analytics.

## Responsibilities

My responsibilities included:

- Conducting a comprehensive review of the existing data architecture.
- Analysing Azure infrastructure utilisation and associated operational costs.
- Reviewing data pipelines, reporting workflows and governance processes.
- Producing recommendations covering architecture, data modelling, observability and CI/CD.
- Working with stakeholders to balance technical improvements against business priorities.
- Developing a phased modernisation roadmap that could be implemented incrementally.

## Major achievements

Key outcomes included:

- Identified significant opportunities to reduce infrastructure expenditure through architectural improvements.
- Designed a modern cloud-native analytics architecture centred on scalable storage, analytics engineering principles and improved governance.
- Produced recommendations to improve data quality, platform reliability and developer productivity.
- Defined a roadmap introducing semantic modelling, observability, automated testing and deployment practices.
- Evaluated the existing data estate for AI readiness, highlighting the improvements required before LLM-driven analytics could be adopted effectively.

## Leadership

Although operating as an individual consultant, the role required influencing senior stakeholders across technical and business teams and mentoring junior product managers, data engineers and data scientists. Success depended on communicating complex architectural concepts clearly, building consensus around the future direction of the platform, and producing recommendations that balanced technical excellence with commercial realities.
