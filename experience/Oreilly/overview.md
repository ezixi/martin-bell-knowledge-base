# Safari Books Online (O'Reilly Media)

## Summary

As Insight & Analysis Lead, I established data-driven decision making across product, marketing and digital acquisition for one of the world's largest online technical learning platforms. Working with teams responsible for millions of monthly users, I developed the analytics capabilities that enabled product optimisation, customer acquisition and long-term growth.

This role marked the beginning of my transition from digital marketing into data leadership, combining technical analytics with commercial strategy to improve business performance.

## Objectives

The primary objectives of the role were to:

- Improve understanding of customer behaviour across digital products.
- Establish reliable analytics across web, mobile and internal systems.
- Increase customer acquisition through data-driven optimisation.
- Introduce experimentation as a core product and marketing capability.
- Provide actionable insight to product and commercial stakeholders.

## Responsibilities

My responsibilities included:

- Leading analytics and reporting across product and marketing functions.
- Delivering a company-wide Google Analytics implementation.
- Developing customer acquisition and SEO strategies.
- Building experimentation programmes using A/B and multivariate testing.
- Partnering with product managers and marketing teams to improve customer conversion and retention.
- Producing insights that informed strategic product decisions.

## Major achievements

Key achievements included:

- Led the implementation of Google Analytics across websites, mobile applications and internal tools.
- Developed an SEO strategy that increased organic traffic from almost zero to millions of page views per month.
- Created a dedicated acquisition optimisation function focused on experimentation and continuous improvement.
- Introduced A/B and multivariate testing that delivered double-digit improvements in trial sign-up and conversion rates.
- Established analytics as a key input into both product and marketing decision making.

## Leadership

This role required significant cross-functional leadership. I worked closely with product managers, engineers, marketers and executives to embed data into everyday decision making and establish a culture of experimentation across the organisation. I mentored junior analysts and data engineers, helping them improve their technical and soft skills.
