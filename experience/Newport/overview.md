# Newport Takkt

## Summary

As Data Engineering Lead, I was responsible for designing and delivering a modern analytics platform that unified reporting across seven independent e-commerce businesses. Working closely with analysts and business stakeholders, I transformed fragmented reporting processes into a scalable cloud-based analytics environment that enabled consistent decision making across the organisation.

This role marked a transition from primarily delivering technical solutions to shaping analytics architecture, engineering standards, and cross-functional collaboration.

## Objectives

The primary objectives of the role were to:

- Replace fragmented reporting with a unified analytics platform.
- Consolidate data from multiple operational systems.
- Improve reporting consistency across several businesses.
- Increase confidence in business reporting.
- Introduce modern engineering practices into the analytics function.

## Responsibilities

My responsibilities included:

- Designing and implementing the organisation's Google BigQuery data warehouse.
- Developing scalable data pipelines to integrate multiple business systems.
- Working with analysts and stakeholders to understand reporting requirements.
- Defining data models that supported consistent business reporting.
- Introducing engineering best practices into analytics development.
- Automating reporting workflows to improve reliability and reduce manual effort.

## Major achievements

Key achievements included:

- Architected and implemented a cloud-based analytics platform using Google BigQuery.
- Unified reporting across seven e-commerce businesses.
- Automated data pipelines that improved reporting speed and reliability, reducing time to insight from days to minutes.
- Introduced software engineering practices that increased development quality and maintainability.
- Enabled faster and more consistent business decision making through trusted reporting.

## Leadership

Although this was an individual contributor role, I worked closely with analysts, business stakeholders and technical teams to define requirements, influence architectural decisions and establish engineering standards. The role required balancing technical implementation with business priorities while helping shape how analytics engineering operated across the organisation.
